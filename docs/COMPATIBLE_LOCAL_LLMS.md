# MAVE Certified Compatible Local LLMs (With & Without AirLLM)

Only verified, quality-controlled models listed in this catalog and [compatible_models.json](file:///c:/Users/ryzen/Documents/antigravity/dazzling-archimedes/src/main/local/compatible_models.json) can be selected or installed in MAVE. Models that exceed a computer's available GPU/RAM or free disk space are automatically blocked by MAVE and synced with Firebase device capability records (`withAirllm` vs `withoutAirllm`).

> [!NOTE]
> **What is AirLLM?** Instead of loading an entire model into GPU VRAM at once, AirLLM splits huge 30B–70B+ models into individual transformer layers and streams them one layer at a time from disk through your GPU. Inference is slower (`~0.5–4 tok/s`), requires enough free SSD disk space to store the weights, and lets modest 4–8 GB GPUs run models that otherwise require 48+ GB of VRAM.
